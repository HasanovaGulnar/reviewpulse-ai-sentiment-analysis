# Outside-reader check

The writeup is only useful if someone who was not in the room can follow it.
This file records that check: who read it, what they understood, and what they
had to ask about.

**Public case study:**
<https://hasanovagulnar.github.io/reviewpulse-ai-sentiment-analysis/>

## Rules for this check

- The reader must not be the mentor and must not have seen the presentation.
- Do not explain the project first. Send the link and the three questions below.
- Ask for the answer in their own words; do not offer multiple choice.
- A useful negative answer ("I did not understand what neutral means") is worth
  more than a polite "looks good".

## Message to send

> Could you read this short case study and tell me three things? It is a project
> writeup, about five minutes of reading: https://hasanovagulnar.github.io/reviewpulse-ai-sentiment-analysis/
>
> 1. In one sentence, what does the app do and who is it for?
> 2. Looking at the architecture diagram, what happens between a user pressing
>    "Analyze" and seeing a label?
> 3. Was there any part you had to read twice, or a term you would not expect a
>    non-specialist to know?
